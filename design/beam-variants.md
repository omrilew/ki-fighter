# Ki effect concepts

The concept sheet compares three effects. Rippling Plasma is the current Kamehameha effect. Its blue outer layers travel along a stable white core.

Braided Current and Pulsing Surge are available as references for future attacks or characters. Braided Current wraps crossing streams around the core. Pulsing Surge sends distinct pressure bands toward a swelling tip.
