# Alternate Z saga: story planning only

## Approved story decisions (locked)
- The Raditz encounter follows the original ending: Goku restrains Raditz, and Piccolo's Special Beam Cannon kills **both Goku and Raditz**.
- Both subsequently reach King Kai's planet and train there together. Raditz gradually reforms, becomes good-aligned, and ultimately joins the Z Fighters.
- Preserve the tension: Raditz starts ruthless and proud; he does **not** become good instantly. Goku's compassion and shared hardship make his redemption earned.
- Do not rewrite the death scene into an early sacrifice or spare either character.
- The mechanism for Raditz being allowed to travel Snake Way, and the eventual revival/return, are not yet decided; develop options without treating them as canon.


## Provisional chapter structure
1. Saiyan arrival and Raditz encounter: establish conflict and stakes; branch point for Goku/Raditz fate.
2. Otherworld training: Goku and Raditz meet King Kai, rivalry becomes trust through trials and shared sacrifice.
3. Saiyan invasion: Raditz's return/allegiance has consequences for Vegeta, Nappa and the defenders.
4. Namek and Frieza: choices about Saiyan history, loyalty and redemption; Raditz's relationship to Vegeta.
5. Androids and Cell: expanded party relationships and alternate survivor consequences.
6. Majin Buu: the cost of redemption, power and responsibility; ending flags.

## Chapter 1–2: Approved narrative outline

### Chapter 1: The Brother from Space
- Raditz arrives and demands that Goku abandon Earth; Goku refuses.
- Piccolo and Goku fight Raditz. Gohan's outburst creates an opening.
- Goku restrains Raditz; Piccolo fires the Special Beam Cannon through both of them. Both die.
- The original dramatic outcome remains intact; Raditz's redemption begins **after** death.

### Chapter 2: Rivals on Snake Way
- Goku is sent toward King Kai. Raditz is granted a conditional opportunity to accompany him; the authority and conditions are still a story choice.
- Early encounters emphasize mistrust and resentment: Raditz initially views compassion as weakness.
- Training trials demand cooperation rather than brute force; Raditz repeatedly fails before beginning to change.
- Their shared training builds an uneasy brotherhood. Goku chooses to give Raditz a chance; Raditz must choose to earn it.
- The chapter ends with Raditz making a meaningful selfless choice without expecting reward, but his route back to the living world remains unresolved.
