# Alternate Z saga: story planning only

## Approved user premise
Raditz eventually trains with Goku on King Kai's planet, reforms and becomes a good-aligned ally. Preserve this divergence and write consequences rather than reverting to canon.

## Provisional chapter structure
1. Saiyan arrival and Raditz encounter: establish conflict and stakes; branch point for Goku/Raditz fate.
2. Otherworld training: Goku and Raditz meet King Kai, rivalry becomes trust through trials and shared sacrifice.
3. Saiyan invasion: Raditz's return/allegiance has consequences for Vegeta, Nappa and the defenders.
4. Namek and Frieza: choices about Saiyan history, loyalty and redemption; Raditz's relationship to Vegeta.
5. Androids and Cell: expanded party relationships and alternate survivor consequences.
6. Majin Buu: the cost of redemption, power and responsibility; ending flags.

## Major decisions requiring player-author approval
- Goku and Raditz's exact deaths/afterlife path and how Raditz is revived.
- Vegeta's redemption pace, Nappa's fate, Piccolo's role, Gohan's growth.
- Namek/Frieza outcomes and transformations.
- Android 17/18, Future Trunks and Cell outcomes.
- Buu arc resolution and who gets the decisive victory.

## Deliverables after choices
Branching event graph, episode quests, companion unlocks, boss states, dialogue outlines, consequences and save flags. Original dialogue only. Never treat unapproved alternative events as fixed.
